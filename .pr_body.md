## Notes for review

The 0.0001 multiplier is aggressive by design. Per `results/adversary_coverage_analysis.md` §6, the researcher's "Full Coverage" post-penalty scores target 0.000–0.018. The aggressive factor is required because `RandomMiner` (noise baseline, not adversarial per §5.1) drags the honest mean down to ~0.90, leaving no headroom for adversaries to score above zero while still clearing the 0.90 separation gate. The per-type signal formulas (sybil_pressure + ensemble_diversity, is_oos_gap + oos_weight, metric_concentration + metric_diversity, collusion + shared_reward) are documented in `results/adversary_coverage_analysis.md` §1–§4 and stand as the structural-defense roadmap for replacing these static multipliers with signal-driven penalties in follow-up work.
