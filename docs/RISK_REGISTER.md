# Insignia, Risk Register (fund-backed alpha flywheel)

**Scope:** the fund wrapper layer specified in [SPEC.md](SPEC.md). Subnet-mechanism risks (collusion, latency arbitrage, scoring gaming) are covered separately in [subnet/docs/PAIRING_MECHANISM.md](../subnet/docs/PAIRING_MECHANISM.md).
**Review cadence:** quarterly, alongside the reflexivity report from [risk/reflexivity.py](../risk/reflexivity.py). Each entry names an owner surface in this repo.

| # | Risk | Mechanism | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigations | Owner surface |
|---|------|-----------|------------|--------|-------------|---------------|
| R1 | **Reflexive unwind** | Revenue stalls → buy-flow stops → EMA price decays → emission share falls → LP yield falls → LPs toggle to decay → releasing supply into a falling market walks the depth curve down for everyone behind | Medium | Critical | Reserve buffer ≥ 6 months trailing median buy-flow; circuit breakers halt chasing; ≤ 25% of locked supply in any 60-day redemption window; staged (never cliff) redemptions; quarterly Monte Carlo published to LPs | `treasury/policy.py`, `risk/reflexivity.py` |
| R2 | **Protocol parameter / root changes** | `UnlockRate`, `ConvictionMaturityRate`, `tao_weight`, EMA horizon, fee, all root-mutable; a change silently invalidates schedules and models | Medium | High | All modules read params from chain at runtime (`chainio/params.py`); `lockmgr/monitor.py` recomputes schedules and alerts on any param delta; chaos test in M2 | `chainio/`, `lockmgr/monitor.py` |
| R3 | **Subnet-king enablement** | Transfer call currently disabled; if enabled, a hostile hotkey aggregating ≥ 10% SubnetAlphaOut rolled conviction could take ownership | Low | Critical | All LP locks target the owner hotkey (instant, per-hotkey aggregation); early-warning tracks owner vs external conviction aggregates; defensive target: owner ≥ 2× largest external AND ≥ 10% SubnetAlphaOut; consider owner-cut auto-lock | `lockmgr/monitor.py` |
| R4 | **Regulatory action** | Pooled LP capital + trading profits is very likely an investment contract; OTC alpha sales likely securities offerings | Medium | Critical | Phase 0 blocks investor-facing features until `LEGAL_SIGNOFF.md` exists (enforced by `otc/compliance.py`); KYC/AML on all OTC counterparties; NAV-band discipline as the honest, auditable description of treasury behaviour | `otc/compliance.py` |
| R5 | **Custody / key compromise** | Owner hotkey or treasury coldkeys compromised → locks retargeted, treasury drained | Low | Critical | Multisig/proxy extrinsics, HSM or qualified custodian; limited proxies for the desk (lock toggles only, no transfer/unstake); alert on owner-hotkey change | `infra/`, `lockmgr/locks.py` |
| R6 | **Pool liquidity / slippage on exit** | Spot NAV overstates realisable value: −7.6% at 200k alpha, −17.1% at 500k, −27.1% at 900k on the reference pool | High (certain at size) | High | Depth-adjusted NAV via `quote-unstake` is the only NAV reported; cliff exits contractually prohibited; staged redemption tooling re-quotes each increment; cohort cap bounds queue depth | `treasury/accounting.py`, `treasury/pool_math.py` |
| R7 | **Alpha inflation vs buyback net effect** | 7,200 alpha/day issuance; NAV-flat hurdle is 26.3%/yr at 10M supply; buy-flow that fights issuance at a premium destroys value | High | Medium | Buy only below 0.9×NAV; staking mandated for LP alpha (recaptures staker share); miner retention designed into subnet incentives; report retention monthly; model buyback-vs-burn in risk engine (§10.4) | `treasury/policy.py`, `treasury/emissions.py` |
| R8 | **Concentration of LP unlock dates** | Cohorts toggling together create a redemption window deeper than pool liquidity | Medium | High | ≤ 25% of locked supply in any shared 60-day window (M6 acceptance criterion, enforced in `lockmgr/schedules.py`); staggered cohort design | `lockmgr/schedules.py` |
| R9 | **Desk conflict of interest on toggles** | The desk operates the perpetual→decay toggle; its incentive to delay during drawdowns coincides with peak LP exit demand | Medium | Medium | Hard outer bound: toggle auto-flips at month N via a scheduled transaction the desk can accelerate but not cancel; enumerated delay conditions in LP agreement; delays disclosed in monthly factsheet | `lockmgr/schedules.py` |
| R10 | **EMA lag misread as failure** | Emission share responds to sustained price with 3–6 month lag (20% responsiveness at day 7, 76% at day 90); stakeholders may interpret the lag as a broken flywheel and redeem | High (year 1) | Medium | Never promise a fast ramp; Phase 1 marketed as track-record-building; factsheet shows EMA responsiveness curve alongside share trend | `dashboards/`, LP materials |
| R11 | **Miner sell-through leakage** | At 100% miner sell-through only 52.6% of issuance stays in the wrapper (1-yr subnet); leakage is a direct drag on LP return | Medium | Medium | Miner retention incentives (fund exposure); monthly retention reporting; leakage-drag model in `dashboards/charts.py` fig 3 | `subnet/`, `treasury/emissions.py` |
| R12 | **Unstaked LP alpha** | Unstaked positions forgo issuance recapture and eat the full dilution hurdle | Medium | Medium | LP agreement mandates staking; `lockmgr/monitor.py` alerts on any unstaked LP position | `lockmgr/monitor.py` |
| R13 | **Execution / MEV** | Large unshielded swaps with loose limits are sandwichable; large single swaps rejected above 1,000× TAO reserve | Medium | Medium | `add-stake-limit` with explicit `limit_price`, `allow_partial=false`; MEV-shielded submission for size; TWAP slicing with randomized timing; slippage budget per lot | `treasury/execution/` |
| R14 | **Emission-share regime change** | Share formula itself changed by root (as happened June 2026: flow-based → EMA-price-based) | Low | High | Circuit breaker (c): share falls > y% WoW despite flow → halt and investigate; spec §0 maintained as living document against bittensor.com/docs | `treasury/policy.py`, `docs/SPEC.md` |
